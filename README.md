# catlas

**C**onditioning **A**s a **T**heory of **L**earned **A**ttention, **S**emantics and more

This repository serves as a central hub for projects related to my work on
**associative learning theories of attention, schema induction**, and potentially
some more. The overarching aim is to develop and evaluate theoretical and computational
accounts of how learned associations shape attentional allocation and the acquisition
of structured knowledge.

## Inspirations

* [Paskewitz & Jones (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmp.2022.102728) present
model of *derived attention* based on Bayesian hierarchical regression, accounting
for phenomena such as learned predictiveness or backward blocking.
* [Le Pelley et al. (2016)](http://dx.doi.org/10.1037/bul0000064) provide a comprehensive
review of evidence base supporting learned predictiveness accounts of attention,
and propose a model of *derived attention* based on their findings.
* [Foster & Jones (2017)](https://doi.org/10.48550/arXiv.1712.10070) introduce a
model of schema induction guided by reinforcement learning
